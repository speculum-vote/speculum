1 · Where campaign money comes from

Receipts of each member's principal campaign committee this cycle (FEC), through {{ (d.campaign.values()|map(attribute='through')|max) if d.campaign else '—' }}. Small gifts are individual gifts under $200, which campaigns need not itemize. PACs include every political committee that is not a party. Other is the rest of total receipts, such as transfers from joint fundraising committees (which pass on money raised from individuals and PACs together), loans and refunds. Members with no campaign committee on file this cycle are left out, so a party's member count here can be below its seats.
