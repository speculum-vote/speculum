The program's rules shut out businesses "primarily engaged in political or lobbying activities" (13 CFR 120.110(r)), applied to PPP from April 2020. From December 27, 2020, the Economic Aid Act let trade associations and other 501(c)(6) organizations borrow if lobbying was no more than 15% of their receipts and of their activities, and their lobbying cost no more than $1 million in the most recent tax year. The SBA said that cost could be the figure reported under the Lobbying Disclosure Act. This page lists what each organization reported and what it borrowed. It does not decide whether any loan met the rules: the 15% tests can't be checked from these records, and a tax year may not be the calendar year.
