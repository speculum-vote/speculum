Explore the money by industry

Pick an industry to see which members of Congress its lobbying organizations funded, where on the committees that money concentrates, and which organizations gave the most. Filter by party, chamber, state or committee.

What this counts: contributions that lobbying organizations report on LD-203 filings (their PACs and their lobbyists' own gifts) to members sitting now, 2025–2026, grouped by the industry each organization is sorted into under the rules on the Method page. Giving to a member is legal and common. A contribution is not evidence of a vote, a position or a decision.
