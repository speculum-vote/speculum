The rule

Lobbied: named the bill on a lobbying report (LD-2) and files its own reports; lobbying firms are left out. The vote: in each chamber, the last roll call that decided the measure itself. Funded: federal campaign contributions the organization or its PAC reported on LD-203 to a member who voted yea or nay, for {{ years }}; contributions a lobbyist reports as their own are not counted. Both sides: at least one member funded on each side. The full rule →

A lobbying report says an organization worked on a bill, not which side it took. Funding members on both sides is common and lawful and has many explanations. This page records the pattern and leaves the reading to you.
